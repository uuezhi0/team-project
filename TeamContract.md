# Team Contract

---
## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will use discord to communicate outside of class. 

* We will respond within 24 hours of receiving a message. 

* We will notify each other if we will not be showing up to tutorials/lectures or if we cannot meet a deadline.

* We will actively listen to each team members ideas and give everyone a chance to meaningfully contribute

## Decision Making

* We will make decisions collaboratively and discuss until we reach a unanimous vote. 

---
## Conflict resolution

* We will listen to each other's side of the issue and attempt to reach a compromise. We will consult a TA/instructor if a resolution cannot be reached. 

---

## Accountability

* We will complete our shares of the work in a timely manner. We will consult each other, a TA or instructors if we are stuck. 
---
